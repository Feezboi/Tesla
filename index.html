About TESLA

Tesla is a technology and automotive company focused on electric vehicles, energy generation and energy storage.

Tesla was founded in 2003 by engineers who wanted to demonstrate that electric vehicles could provide an alternative to gasoline-powered transportation. The company later expanded from vehicles into energy generation and storage
